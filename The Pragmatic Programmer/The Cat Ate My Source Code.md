## Taking Responsibility
Take responsibility for yourself and your actions, don't be afraid to admit ignorance or error.

No matter how perfect something is, there will always be some unexpected problem around the corner.

If you make a mistake or anything, then deal with them by being honest, direct and as professionally as possible.

It's okay to be proud of your abilities but you must also accept your shortcomings - your ignorance and your mistakes.

## Take Responsibility
Make a commitment to ensure that something is done right.

In addition to doing your own personal best, you should analyse the situation for risks beyond your control.

When you accept responsibility for something, you should also expect to be held accountable for it. When you make a mistake or an error (as we all do), you should admit it honestly and offer options.

Don't come up with excuses or blame people or tools or anything. Any and all of these may play a role but it's up to **you** to provide solutions not excuses.

It's up to you to be prepared for any problems that might happen.
## Provide Options, Don't Make Excuses
Before going to someone to give them an excuse for whatever, stop and think about it thoroughly. Try to think about anything else you can try before making an excuse.

Instead of excuses, try to come up with solutions/options. Instead of saying something can't be done, try to explain what *can* be done to salvage the situation and make the best of everything.

Don't be afraid to ask or admit that you need help. Just flush out the lame excuses before saying them out loud and think about ways to make the best of everything.
