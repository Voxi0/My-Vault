Pragmatic programming is like an attitude, a style, a philosophy of approaching problems and their solutions. They think about the bigger picture beyond the immediate problem.

Pragmatic programming stems from a philosophy of pragmatic thinking.

Each developer is unique with their own strengths and weaknesses and preferences and dislikes. However, all pragmatic programmers share many of the following characteristics -
- **Early Adopter / Fast Adapter**: People who loves to try out new things and learn new stuff quickly and integrate it with the rest of their knowledge.
- **Inquisitive**: Asking lots of questions to learn all sorts of things.
- **Critical Thinking**: Not taking things as given without first getting the facts.
- **Realistic**: Understanding the underlying nature of each problem one faces and understanding how difficult they are and how long things will take. This gives one the stamina to keep trying to solve a problem.
- **Jack of All Trades**: People who are familiar with a broad range of techniques and technologies and such so that they can always move on to new areas and challenges easily
## Tips
- Care about your craft.
- Think about your work, always critique your work in real time to come up with improvements.
- Constantly find ways to improve. Try to refine existing skills and learn new things everyday.
- You have agency. If you desire change, then invest time and effort for it. Be proactive and take any opportunities that comes your way.
